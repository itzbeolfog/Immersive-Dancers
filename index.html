Immersive Dancers
← All ModsHomeGalleryRequest a SongDownload
Fabric Mod · Minecraft 1.20.1

Let Minecraft dance.

Immersive Dancers is a mod created by YouTuber Beolf O.G to add some fun to Minecraft. It adds 5 custom music discs packed with awesome hits from legends like Michael Jackson, ItzBixu, KSI and NEFFEX. Every disc gives the mobs of your world its own extremely fun dance steps, and there is a lot to explore that you won't want to miss. There are also 19 custom fun achievements to unlock along the way. Want your own favorite song turned into a dance? Join my Discord server to request it. I'd love to see your reactions, guys!
